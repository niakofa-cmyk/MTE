What should happen after the descendants are extracted?

This is the most important narrative and game-flow decision.

The rescue should be the end of the opening mission, not the end of the story.

The descendants' extraction should reveal a larger conflict and give the player a clear reason to continue. The next chapter should take the player from survival and rescue into investigation, alliance-building, and a counteroffensive.

I recommend the following story structure.

Mission 1 — The Rescue

Purpose: Establish the world, the threat, the descendants, and the player's immediate objective.

The current mission already provides the foundations for this chapter:

The player enters the hostile region.
The player fights through security forces.
The player locates and frees the descendants.
The player activates the beacon.
The player reaches extraction.

Before we extend this mission, we must make sure that every step works reliably during normal gameplay.

The mission should end with a cinematic that establishes three things:

The descendants are safe, at least temporarily.
The enemy has discovered that the player has disrupted its operation.
The rescue has uncovered information pointing toward a much larger threat.

The final reveal should come from actual mission content: a recovered transmission, intelligence item, character dialogue, or another narrative asset. We should not pretend that this cinematic already exists unless the repository demonstrates it.
