---
name: Multiplayer scaling roadmap
description: User-set staged player-count goals and separate arena versus planetary warfare modes.
---

The user's roadmap is Phase 1: 8-player 4v4; Phase 2: 16-player 8v8; Phase 3: 40-player 20v20; Phase 4: 100-player planetary warfare; and a long-term exploratory target of 100–150 players. Competitive arena and planetary warfare are complementary but different modes.

**Why:** The user asked to design for future scale while delivering and validating one stage at a time; a larger player cap alone does not make a larger mode viable.

**How to apply:** Keep 4v4 as the active Cinder Row profile. For each later phase, add its own map/mode capacity, spawn layout, objective and visibility/network budgets, then verify load and failure behavior before claiming support.
